A newspaper front page that sums up the world's news of
**Sunday 22 November 2026** in a single 1024×1024 image.

## What it covers

- **Lead story — COP31 climate talks collapse in Antalya, Turkey:**
  the UN climate summit ends with no deal after countries fail to
  agree on fossil fuels and climate finance; the EU rejects a final
  text that never names fossil fuels.
- **Ukraine war:** a sea drone is reported to have hit a Russian
  warship in Benghazi port, Libya; Russian missiles strike Kryvyi
  Rih, Zelenskyy's home city — 2 killed, 60 wounded.
- **Middle East:** Iran strikes a civilian plant in Sitra, Bahrain,
  and a tanker near the Strait of Hormuz; a US base in Bahrain is
  also attacked.
- **Mexico:** special forces capture cartel boss "El Chapo Isidro"
  (Fausto Isidro Meza Flores) in Sinaloa; 11 die in the gun battle.
- **Indonesia:** a candy factory fire in Cikande, Banten, kills
  three; dozens are missing, 36 still sought.
- **Markets:** stocks plunge as the AI sell-off spreads — S&P 500
  −3.7%, Nasdaq −5.3%.
- **In brief:** BepiColombo enters orbit around Mercury; the ICJ
  rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in 2017;
  the EU and Indonesia sign a major trade deal; US Democrats win
  at least 258 House seats.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`
(or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` when that variable is set)
and verifies the copy is byte-identical by comparing SHA-256 hashes.
